    The IMAG building

    The workshop will take place in the IMAG building on the main campus of Université Grenoble Alpes on the eastern side of Grenoble. The building is a short walk from the tram stops serving the campus.

    Address:

    Bâtiment IMAG 700 avenue Centrale 38401 Saint-Martin-d'Hères France

    You can find its location here.

    Getting to the campus

    • Tram: Lines B and C serve the Université Grenoble Alpes campus. Get off at Bibliothèques Universitaires or Gabriel Fauré and walk to the IMAG building.

    • Bus: Several city bus lines stop on campus.

    • Bike: Grenoble has an extensive cycle network and a public bike-share scheme (see their website); bike parking is available near the building.

    • Car: Parking on campus is limited; public transport is strongly recommended.

    Getting to Grenoble

    • By train: Grenoble train station (Gare de Grenoble) has direct connections to Lyon (about 1h20), Paris (about 3h), and Geneva (about 2h).

    • By air:

      • Lyon–Saint-Exupéry Airport (LYS) is about 1 hour away by direct shuttle bus or train.

      • Geneva Airport (GVA) is about 2 hours away by coach.

    Summer school

    The summer school on 6 September 2027 will also be held on the Université Grenoble Alpes campus. The exact room will be announced with the program.
