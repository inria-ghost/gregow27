    Scope

    The Grenoble Global Optimization Workshop welcomes contributions on all aspects of global optimization, including but not limited to:

    • deterministic and stochastic methods for global optimization,

    • continuous, discrete, and mixed-integer nonlinear optimization,

    • convex relaxations, bounding, and branch-and-bound techniques,

    • algorithms and open-source software,

    • applications in engineering, energy, life sciences, machine learning, and beyond.

    Contributions from academia, industry, and the public sector are all encouraged.

    Format

    Authors are invited to submit an extended abstract describing original work. A LaTeX template and detailed formatting instructions will be made available on this page.

    All submissions will be reviewed by the scientific committee. Accepted contributions will be scheduled for oral presentation during the workshop.
