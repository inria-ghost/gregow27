# Practical local information

\toc

## Getting around

* **Public transport:** The TAG network runs trams and buses across the
  metropolitan area. You can either buy tickets at machines or via the mobile app,
  or use contactless credit card payments when boarding the tram or bus.
  The campus can be accessed with the **tram B and C lines**.
* **Cycling:** Grenoble is one of the most bike-friendly cities in France. You can rent bikes for several days [from the city](https://www.grenoblealpesmetropole.fr/123-louer-un-velo-une-trotinette.htm) or rent electric bikes or scooters from Voi.

## Food and social life

Grenoble has a lively food scene, from traditional Dauphinois cuisine to a wide
choice of international restaurants, cafés, and bars.

## Things to do

* Walk up the *Bastille*, the fort looking over the city, or use the cable cars ("les Bulles") to enjoy panoramic views.
* The Musée de Grenoble is a major art museum with modern and contemporary collections, while the Musée Dauphinois offers an overview of the region's history.
* Walk or cycle along the Isère and Drac rivers.
* Explore the nearby mountain ranges for hikes, via ferratas, or bike trips.
* The former Chartreuse distillery is in Voiron (accessible by train or buses from Grenoble) and offers visits in French and English.
