# Venue

\toc

## The IMAG building

The workshop will take place in the **IMAG building** on the main campus of
**Université Grenoble Alpes** on the eastern side of Grenoble.
The building is a short walk from the tram stops serving the campus.

Address:

> Bâtiment IMAG
> 700 avenue Centrale
> 38401 Saint-Martin-d'Hères
> France

You can find its location [here](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=45.19056&mlon=5.76728#map=19/45.190560/5.767280&layers=CN).

## Getting to the campus

* **Tram:** Lines B and C serve the Université Grenoble Alpes campus. Get off at
  *Bibliothèques Universitaires* or *Gabriel Fauré* and walk to the IMAG
  building.
* **Bus:** Several city bus lines stop on campus.
* **Bike:** Grenoble has an extensive cycle network and a public bike-share scheme (see their [website](https://www.grenoblealpesmetropole.fr/123-louer-un-velo-une-trotinette.htm)); bike parking is available near the building.
* **Car:** Parking on campus is limited; public transport is strongly recommended.

## Getting to Grenoble

* **By train:** Grenoble train station (Gare de Grenoble) has direct connections
  to Lyon (about 1h20), Paris (about 3h), and Geneva (about 2h).
* **By air:**
  * Lyon–Saint-Exupéry Airport (LYS) is about 1 hour away by direct shuttle bus or train.
  * Geneva Airport (GVA) is about 2 hours away by coach.
