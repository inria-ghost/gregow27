Grenoble Global Optimization Workshop

Université Grenoble Alpes — 7–10 September 2027

The workshop

The Global Optimization Workshop gathers researchers and practitioners working on the theory, algorithms, software, and applications of global optimization — deterministic and stochastic, continuous, discrete, and mixed-integer.

It will be held in Grenoble from the 7th to the 10th of September 2027, at the IMAG building on the main campus of Université Grenoble Alpes.

A summer school on open-source software for global optimization will be organized on Monday, the 6th of September 2027, the day before the workshop.

More information will follow soon on this dedicated website.
